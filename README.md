# Remote Hiring OS

AI-assisted landing page, lead qualification, and CRM automation project developed for the Pareto Talent Executive Assistant Bootcamp.

# 👉 [OPEN THE LIVE LANDING PAGE](https://remote-hiring-os.lovable.app/)

---

## Project Overview

Remote Hiring OS is a hypothetical offer developed as an educational project for the Pareto Talent Executive Assistant Bootcamp.

The project used Kasim Aslam as the sample founder and his Second Brain/interview material as research context. The goal was to move from founder research to a defined offer and then build the system around it: positioning, landing-page strategy, copy, responsive implementation, lead qualification, CRM pipeline, and automated follow-up.

The result is a simple end-to-end funnel designed to turn website visitors into qualified leads and organize their progression through a sales process.

## Process

The project was developed in several stages:

**Founder Research → Offer Development → Landing Page Strategy → Copy & Design → Published Website → Lead Qualification → CRM Pipeline → Workflow Automation**

Rather than treating the landing page as a standalone deliverable, the project evolved into a connected lead-management system.

---

## 1. Founder Research & Offer Development

The project began with research into Kasim Aslam's experience, ideas, and approach to hiring and team building.

ChatGPT was used to help synthesize the source material, identify recurring themes, organize relevant information, and translate the research into a potential offer.

From this process, **Remote Hiring OS** was developed as a hypothetical offer focused on helping small business owners build a more systematic approach to hiring remote talent.

The research informed:

- target audience
- core problem
- offer positioning
- value proposition
- messaging priorities
- landing-page content

---

## 2. Landing Page Strategy & Copy

The research and offer definition were translated into a landing-page structure designed around a clear conversion path.

The page was developed around:

- a focused value proposition
- problem and solution framing
- founder credibility
- offer explanation
- calls to action
- lead qualification

ChatGPT supported the strategy, information architecture, copy development, and iteration process.

---

## 3. Landing Page Implementation

The landing page was built and published with **Lovable**.

The implementation included the page structure, visual system, responsive layout, copy, calls to action, and the application entry point.

## 4. Lead Qualification

A qualification form was created in **GoHighLevel** to collect contact information and capture information about each lead's hiring needs.

The form collects:

- Full Name
- Phone
- Email
- Current hiring situation
- Expected hiring timeline

The qualification questions provide information that can be used to distinguish between immediate hiring needs, medium-term opportunities, and leads who are still exploring remote hiring.

---

## 5. CRM Pipeline

A five-stage pipeline was created in **GoHighLevel** to organize leads throughout the process:

1. **New Application**
2. **Qualified**
3. **Call Booked**
4. **Call Completed**
5. **Enrolled**

This creates a visible progression from initial application to enrollment and provides a structured way to manage opportunities inside the CRM.

---

## 6. Workflow Automation

A workflow was configured in **GoHighLevel** to automate the actions that follow a form submission.

The workflow connects lead capture with CRM organization and follow-up:

**Form Submission → Tag Applied → Opportunity Created → Internal Notification → Confirmation Email → Delay → Follow-up Email**

This reduces the need for manual processing after each application and ensures that new leads enter a consistent follow-up process.

---

## 7. System Architecture

The completed project connects the public-facing experience with the internal lead-management process:

**Visitor**

↓

**Landing Page**

↓

**Qualification Form**

↓

**GoHighLevel CRM**

↓

**Pipeline / Opportunity**

↓

**Automated Follow-up**

This transformed the original landing-page exercise into a small operational funnel combining research, communication, lead management, and automation.
